Shadow Slave Fan Page

Description

Shadow Slave is a stroy following Sunny the main character Sunny is from a very poor live style until he is effected by the Nightmare Spell and takes Sunny to a differnt world or has it seems for now.

Story events

1st Nightmare

The Nightmare that Sunny is forced into is on a mountain that has snow everywhere he looks. Strangely, the sun starts to move from East to West and the snow rises up into the clouds. Sunny then sees the skeletons of a cart, and they come alive. Sunny becomes one of them and realizes that he is a slave wearing a slave collar. He checks his Status page—a page all people affected by the Nightmare Spell possess. For now, he is an Aspirant, which is what people who enter their first Nightmare are called. On his Status page, he sees that his overall role is a Temple Slave. The Spell's judgment describes him as a useless man who is soon to die. He also has several attributes: Fated, which gives him extreme twists of good and horrible luck; Child of Shadow, which gives him an affinity for shadows; and Mark of Divinity (Faded), a very weak mark of divinity on his soul. Sunny is forced up the mountain by the guards watching over the slaves. They stop to camp, but then an Awakened Tyrant—a very powerful being—attacks. Later, Sunny is left with two other slaves and a noble knight named Auro of the Nine. The group heads up the mountain, trying to escape as the monster falls off the peak. One of the slaves dies from injuries and the freezing cold. The next day, the body is gone, letting them know they are being hunted down. Later, the second slave tries to convince Auro to leave Sunny behind so they can get away, but Auro gets rid of the second slave instead. He and Sunny then take shelter in a cave for the night. Auro asks if Sunny is asleep. Sunny is awake and accuses Auro of trying to kill him. Auro apologizes, saying that if it were up to him he wouldn't, but his life isn't only his own. With that, he prepares to attack. Sunny rings his Bell Memory. Confused for a second, Auro doesn't see the monster behind him. Sunny runs away as the two fight, eventually stumbling into a very old, abandoned temple. Bleeding, he lies down on an altar. The system informs him that he has offered himself to the gods, and in their forever sleep of death, the Shadow God gifts him. As the monster approaches, shadows kill it, bringing the Nightmare to an end. After completing the Nightmare, Sunny awakens in a strange space that feels like nothing and everything at once. The Spell evaluates his Aspect as Divine and grants him the True Name Lost from Light. He then wakes up back at the police station, where he was before entering the Nightmare.

Academy

After completing his First Nightmare, Sunny wakes up at the police station and learns that he has become an Awakened. He meets Master Jet, who explains what happens next and helps him get to the Awakened Academy. At the Academy, Sunny spends four weeks learning about the Dream Realm, Nightmare Creatures, Memories, and the different ranks of Awakened. He meets other Sleepers, including Nephis, a blind girl named Cassie he sits with but doesn't relly talk to thinking shes doomed to die and learns from Teacher Julius, who teaches him about survival and the dangers of the Dream Realm. During this time, Sunny also learns more about his Divine Aspect and shadow abilities. He has to keep his True Name a secret because revealing it could put him in danger has anyone that says it can control him like a slave. He also struggles with his Flaw, which makes him unable to lie. When the Winter Solstice arrives, Sunny and the other Sleepers prepare to enter the Dream Realm for their next challenge.

Forggton Shore

Sunny woke up falling and then found himself underwater. He swam up and saw nothing but water. Then he saw a massive monster coming right for him. He swam as fast as he could and got onto a massive rock he found, escaping by the skin of his teeth. He used his ability to send out his shadow to look around. There were monsters and red coral everywhere he looked. At nighttime, he saw a light far away. He then planned to go over there as soon as the Dark Sea disappeared in the morning. The Dark Sea only shows up after the sun goes down. Sunny eventually reached the light and saw two girls he recognized: Nephis and Cassie. He had seen them from far away, so he used his shadow to approach them and wave, making sure Nephis wouldn't stab him on sight because she didn't know he was human. After a while, Sunny showed himself, and the three of them became a group. The three of them stayed the night on a massive stone statue, just like Sunny had during his first night there. In the morning, they went down and started exploring. They had to plan where to stay because, at night, the Dark Sea comes and swallows anything that isn't high enough. Later, one night, Cassie used her ability to dream about the future and described a vision. She saw a lonely star, a burning castle, several mysterious figures facing terrible dangers, and a crimson spire with seven severed heads guarding seven locks. At the top, she saw a dying angel being consumed by hungry shadows. She then turned to Sunny and explained that she felt as though something incredibly precious had been taken from her. They got ready to go back to sleep while thinking about Cassie's future vision. After traveling for days or weeks, they reached the end of the red coral. In front of them was nothing but dry, flat land as far as the eye could see. This was dangerous because the Dark Sea still affected that area. After a while, they used Sunny's Echo to help them. An Echo has a very small chance of appearing when you kill a Nightmare Creature. You can summon an Echo to fight for you. They eventually reached a very beautiful and peaceful-looking tree. Underneath it were mountains of dead bodies belonging to monsters and humans. They sent Sunny's Echo out to fight the monster resting there. After a very hard and deadly fight, they won. They ate some of the monster's meat and some of the fruit from the strange tree. Later, Cassie woke Sunny up and talked about the number five. However, Sunny eventually woke up, believing it had been a weird dream. They stayed there for weeks, happy because everything seemed amazing and peaceful. However, Nephis seemed to be in an upset mood. Sunny tried to make her happy. He climbed the tree and found an egg. He destroyed it, and the Spell informed him that he had killed a Great Devil. He received a golden drop as a reward. Sunny drank the golden drop and experienced extreme amounts of pain. When he woke up, he was shocked because, for a moment, he saw a hex on him in his Status page. He had to focus and make himself feel pain to understand what was happening. Sometimes, he would go to Nephis and tell her three names: Anvil, Ki Song, and Asterion. This made her obey his commands. Every time he said those names, Nephis would become confused but still do what he told her. After a while, they made a boat out of monster bones. Sunny eventually forced Cassie and convinced Nephis to join him. They escaped from the tree, and the mind hex became weaker and weaker.
